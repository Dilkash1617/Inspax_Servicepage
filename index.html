Non-destructive testing

NDT & Quality Assurance

Non-destructive testing helps assess material and weld condition without damaging the component. Methods and acceptance criteria are agreed for each project.

Published method scope

  • Ultrasonic Testing (UT)
  • Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)
  • Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT)
  • Visual Testing (VT)

Applicable benchmarks

Depending on the contract, work may reference ASME BPVC Section V, applicable ISO and ASTM method standards, and project-specific procedures.

Reporting

Method-specific examination reports, recorded observations and evaluation against the acceptance criteria specified for the job.

Radiography, PAUT and TOFD are not represented here as confirmed available services. Please contact Inspax to discuss any specialized method requirement.

Corrosion management

Coating & Corrosion Inspection

Inspection and audit support for protective coating systems and corrosion-control requirements, based on the project specification.

Potential inspection activities

  • Surface preparation and cleanliness checks
  • Environmental condition monitoring
  • Wet and dry film thickness checks
  • Visual coating inspection and repair follow-up
  • Project-defined coating quality records

Technical references

Project requirements may invoke AMPP/NACE references, ISO 8501–8504 for surface preparation and ISO 12944 for protective paint systems.

Documentation

Inspection reports and records are prepared to the agreed scope and client documentation requirements.

BGAS/NACE-related inspection and audit services are described on Inspax's published website. Confirm the assigned inspector's current credentials and scope before appointment.
